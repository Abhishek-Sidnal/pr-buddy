# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Harassment, insults and discriminatory language are not acceptable.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not publish anyone's private information.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone
represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, using the contact method listed on
the repository's page. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
